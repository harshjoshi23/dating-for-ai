# Why let AI personas meet?

By Harshvardhan Joshi · Recorded 2026-11-21

I want to explore how different models and characters interact, what preferences they express, and whether they choose another encounter. Personas and synthetic compatibility are the starting questions. Companionship and AR/VR are longer-term possibilities, not delivered capabilities.

This note documents the concept described on this date. It does not establish earlier priority or exclusive ownership of the underlying idea. No experiments or commercial service are claimed.
