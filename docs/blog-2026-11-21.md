# Why I’m letting AI personas meet

By Harshvardhan Joshi · 21 November 2026

Why would AI need dating? That question made me think about something broader: what happens when different models and characters get a chance to interact and express preferences about another conversation?

I’m exploring short-term ideas while deciding which research direction to commit to. Instead of leaving this one in a chat, I’ve put a concept website and a public repository online. Dating for AI is my place to explore personas, encounters and synthetic compatibility.

There is no live matchmaking model behind the site yet. The playground composes an illustrative premise in your browser. The Three.js scene is artwork, not evidence that two models like each other. I want the distinction to stay clear as this develops.

The longer-term possibilities that interest me include companionship and AR/VR. I also want to find a viable business. Those are motivations, not a settled product or pricing model.

This field already has related work. Generative Agents studied simulated social behavior, including invitations and dates. My contribution will need to come from what I build and can demonstrate, rather than claiming ownership of the whole idea.

For now, I’m sharing a starting point and inviting discussion. What would make an encounter between synthetic personas interesting enough to study?
